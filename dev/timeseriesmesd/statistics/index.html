PlanetPostStats.TimeSeriesMeSd.PeriodMeans — Type
PeriodMeans

Temporal means over periods (years or occurrences of one season) of the gridded field and of its zonal and global means, with availability weights (fraction of valid months of the period; 0 where the threshold is not met). Returned by annual_means and seasonal_means.

Fields

  • ij, ij_w: (Lon, Lat, [Lev], Period).
  • zm, zm_w: (1, Lat, [Lev], Period).
  • gm, gm_w: (1, 1, [Lev], Period).

Example

an = annual_means(x, sm, months, w; threshold=80)
size(an.gm, ndims(an.gm))     # number of years

PlanetPostStats.TimeSeriesMeSd.SpatialMeans — Type
SpatialMeans

Zonal and global means of a monthly field with their availability weights, as returned by planet_means.

Fields

  • zm, zm_w: zonal mean and fraction of valid longitudes, (1, Lat, [Lev], Time).
  • gm, gm_w: area-weighted global mean and valid area fraction, (1, 1, [Lev], Time).

Availabilities are 0 where the threshold is not met (the mean is then NaN).

Example

sm = planet_means(x, area; threshold=80)
size(sm.gm)

PlanetPostStats.TimeSeriesMeSd.annual_means — Method
annual_means(x, sm, months, month_weights; threshold=80.0) -> PeriodMeans

Annual means of all complete calendar years of the monthly record, for the gridded field x and its zonal and global means sm, computed with PlanetTools.PlanetStatistics.calc_annual_mean_ts!.

Definitions (PlanetTools): month t gets the weight month_weights[t] times the availability of its input (1 for the gridded field; sm.zm_w, sm.gm_w for the zonal and global means, i.e. months with partly missing spatial coverage get less weight); the mean is NaN if fewer than threshold percent of the 12 months are valid; the availability of the annual mean is the fraction of valid months.

Arguments

  • x::AbstractArray{<:Real}: monthly field (Lon, Lat, [Lev], Time).
  • sm::SpatialMeans: its zonal and global means from planet_means.
  • months::AbstractVector{YearMonth}: labels of the Time dimension (consecutive).
  • month_weights: weights of the months (length Time), e.g. month_weights(calendar, months), or nothing for equal weights.

Keyword arguments

  • threshold::Real = 80.0: minimum percentage of valid months.

Returns

PeriodMeans with one entry per year of annual_periods(months); an empty last dimension if there is no complete year.

Example

w = month_weights(MARS_CALENDAR, months)
an = annual_means(x, planet_means(x, area), months, w)

PlanetPostStats.TimeSeriesMeSd.multiyear_stats — Method
multiyear_stats(x, x_w, slots; threshold=80.0, corrected=true) -> (mean, sd, avail)

Multi-year mean and standard deviation over the contiguous samples slots (last dimension of x, e.g. the annual or seasonal means of the years in the statistics window), computed with PlanetTools.PlanetStatistics.calc_multiyear_stats! (Val(:annual)).

Definitions (PlanetTools): with the availabilities x_w as weights w of the valid samples, V₁ = Σw, V₂ = Σw², the mean is μ = Σwx / V₁ and the standard deviation

  • corrected=true (default): σ = √(Σw(x − μ)² / (V₁ − V₂/V₁)), the sample standard deviation for reliability weights, √(Σ(x − μ)² / (n − 1)) for equal weights; NaN with fewer than two valid samples;
  • corrected=false: the population standard deviation √(Σw(x − μ)² / V₁).

Results are NaN if fewer than threshold percent of the samples are valid; avail is the fraction of valid samples.

Arguments

  • x::AbstractArray{<:Real}: samples along the last dimension.
  • x_w: availability weights of x (same size) or nothing (equal weights).
  • slots::AbstractUnitRange{<:Integer}: indices of the samples to use.

Keyword arguments

  • threshold::Real = 80.0: minimum percentage of valid samples.
  • corrected::Bool = true: sample (true) or population (false) standard deviation.

Example

julia> me, sd, av = multiyear_stats(reshape(Float32[1, 2, 3, NaN], 1, 4), nothing, 1:4;
                                    threshold=50);

julia> me[1], sd[1], av[1]
(2.0f0, 1.0f0, 0.75f0)

PlanetPostStats.TimeSeriesMeSd.planet_means — Method
planet_means(x, area; threshold=80.0) -> SpatialMeans

Zonal means and area-weighted global means of the monthly field x, computed with PlanetTools.PlanetStatistics.calc_planet_means!.

Definitions (PlanetTools): the zonal mean averages the valid longitudes with equal weights, its availability is the fraction of valid longitudes; the global mean is weighted by the cell areas area, its availability is the valid area fraction. A mean is NaN (availability 0) if the availability is below threshold percent.

The PlanetTools kernel loops over the data without temporary arrays and is multi-threaded over levels and time steps.

Arguments

  • x::AbstractArray{<:Real}: (Lon, Lat, Time) or (Lon, Lat, Lev, Time), missing values NaN.
  • area::AbstractMatrix{<:Real}: cell areas (Lon, Lat) (axyp).

Keyword arguments

  • threshold::Real = 80.0: minimum valid percentage (longitudes or area).

Example

julia> x = reshape(Float32[1, 3, 2, NaN], 2, 2, 1);

julia> sm = planet_means(x, [1.0 3.0; 1.0 3.0]; threshold=0);

julia> vec(sm.zm), sm.gm[1], sm.gm_w[1]
(Float32[2.0, 2.0], 2.0f0, 0.625f0)

PlanetPostStats.TimeSeriesMeSd.seasonal_means — Method
seasonal_means(x, sm, months, season, month_weights; threshold=80.0) -> PeriodMeans

Means over all complete occurrences of season in the monthly record, for the gridded field x and its zonal and global means sm, computed with PlanetTools.PlanetStatistics.calc_seasonal_mean_ts!. Weighting, availability, and threshold are defined as for annual_means, with the months of the season in place of the 12 months of a year.

Arguments

  • x, sm, months: as for annual_means.
  • season::SeasonSpec: the season.
  • month_weights: weights of the months, or nothing for equal weights.

Keyword arguments

  • threshold::Real = 80.0: minimum percentage of valid months of a season.

Returns

PeriodMeans with one entry per element of seasonal_periods(months, season).

Example

se = seasonal_means(x, sm, months, SeasonSpec("FMA", 2, 3), w)
