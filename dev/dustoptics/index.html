PlanetPostStats.DustOptics — Module
DustOptics

Submodule of PlanetPostStats for the optical post-processing of the dust aerosol of ModelE/ROCKE-3D simulations. Its input are the monthly time series of the dust tracer mass mixing ratios and of the air mass of the model layers written by TimeSeriesMeSd (e.g. *.ijl.mts.nc of the dust tracers Clay, Silt1, …). For every run and month it computes

  • the dust mass of every size bin of the radiative calculations in every layer, m_b = f_b q_s m_air, from the mass mixing ratio q_s of its source tracer, the fraction f_b of the tracer in the bin (e.g. the split of the clay tracer into four sub-bins), and the air mass m_air of the layer (DustBin, DustBinMasses);
  • the effective radius of the size distribution in the layers, of the column dust load, and its mass-weighted column average (effective_radius, column_mean_effective_radius);
  • the scattering, absorption, and extinction optical depths τ = k m_b of every bin and of the cumulative size classes (SumClay1Dust, …, PM2Dust, …, PM32Dust) in the layers and of the column, at selected diagnostic wavelengths, with the mass coefficients k interpolated from the optical lookup tables of the radiation code to the wavelengths and the effective radii of the bins (SpectralTable, SpectralSet, interpolate_coefficients).

All these variables are then written as time series, multi-year means, and standard deviations of gridded, zonal-mean, and global-mean fields with the pipeline of TimeSeriesMeSd (process_runs): one run at a time, and one variable at a time, since every variable is a LazyVariable computed from the bin masses only when it is written. The memory needed is that of the source tracer masses of one run plus a few fields.

All settings are collected in a DustOpticsConfig; the entry point is run_dust_optics.

Example

using PlanetPostStats.TimeSeriesMeSd
using PlanetPostStats.DustOptics

stats = TimeSeriesConfig(; simpath="/path/to/simulations", runs=["MarsDustRun1"],
    sim_period=(YearMonth(1, 1), YearMonth(20, 12)),
    stat_period=(YearMonth(3, 1), YearMonth(20, 12)), calendar=MARS_CALENDAR,
    seasons=MARS_SEASONS, vertical=VerticalCoordinate(31, 6.86, 0.2),
    output_dir="Traceroptics3d", output_name="traceroptics3d")
bins = [DustBin("Clay1"; source="Clay", radius=0.132e-6, density=2.5e3),
        DustBin("Clay2"; source="Clay", radius=0.23e-6, density=2.5e3),
        DustBin("Clay3"; source="Clay", radius=0.416e-6, density=2.5e3),
        DustBin("Clay4"; source="Clay", radius=0.766e-6, density=2.5e3),
        DustBin("Silt1"; radius=1.386e-6, density=2.65e3)]
sw = SpectralSet("sw", read_spectral_table("aer_sw_dust.nc", "sw_bands.csv"),
                 [0.55e-6, 0.89e-6])
cfg = DustOpticsConfig(; stats=stats, bins=bins, spectral=[sw],
    fractions=bin_fractions(bins, "Clay" => [0.009, 0.081, 0.234, 0.676]),
    input_dir="Tracermass3d", input_name="tracermass3d")
results = run_dust_optics(cfg)
annual_summary(results)

PlanetPostStats.DustOptics.DustBin — Type
DustBin(name; source=name, radius, density)

One size bin of the dust aerosol as used in the optical calculations.

The mass of the bin is a fixed fraction of the mass of one transported dust tracer, its source: several bins may share a source, e.g. the four sub-bins Clay1, …, Clay4 into which the clay tracer Clay of ModelE/ROCKE-3D is split for the radiative transfer, while every silt bin is its own source (Silt1 from Silt1, …). The fractions are given per run (DustOpticsConfig.fractions, bin_fractions).

Arguments

  • name::AbstractString: name of the bin, used in the output variable names (e.g. ext3d_550nm_Clay1).

Keyword arguments

  • source::AbstractString = name: name of the dust tracer (mass mixing ratio) in the input files that contains the mass of this bin.
  • radius::Real: effective radius of the bin (m), used for the effective radius of the size distribution and to interpolate the optical properties to the bin.
  • density::Real: particle density (kg m⁻³).

Example

julia> b = DustBin("Clay1"; source="Clay", radius=0.132e-6, density=2.5e3);

julia> b.source, b.radius
("Clay", 1.32e-7)

PlanetPostStats.DustOptics.DustBinMasses — Type
DustBinMasses(sources, masses, source_index, fractions)

Dust masses per unit area in the model layers of one run, held once per source tracer: the mass of bin b is fractions[b] .* masses[source_index[b]].

Fields

  • sources::Vector{String}: names of the source tracers.
  • masses::Vector{Array{Float32,4}}: mass per unit area of each source tracer (kg m⁻²), (lon, lat, level, time), i.e. the mass mixing ratio times the air mass of the layer; missing values NaN32.
  • source_index::Vector{Int}: index into sources of the source of every bin.
  • fractions::Vector{Float64}: fraction of the source mass in every bin.

Linear combinations of the bin masses, Σ_b c_b m_b, are computed with combine_bins, so that the per-bin fields never have to be stored.

Example

m = DustBinMasses(["Clay"], [rand(Float32, 4, 3, 2, 12)], [1, 1], [0.4, 0.6])
size(combine_bins(m, [1.0, 1.0]))      # (4, 3, 2, 12): total mass of the two bins

PlanetPostStats.DustOptics.DustOpticsConfig — Type
DustOpticsConfig(; stats, bins, input_dir, input_name, kwargs...)

All settings of the optical post-processing of dust, run_dust_optics.

The input are the monthly time series of the dust tracer mass mixing ratios and of the air mass of the layers written by PlanetPostStats.TimeSeriesMeSd (<prefix>.<input_name><run>.<input_tag>.mts.nc in <input_root>/<run>/<input_dir>/MonthlyTs/). From these, for every run and month, the dust masses of the size bins in the model layers are formed, and from them

  • the effective radius of the dust in the layers (effRad3d_<class>), of the column dust load (effRadColMass_<class>), and its mass-weighted column average (effRadColAve_<class>), where <class> is the largest size class of optics_classes (e.g. PM32Dust), or Dust for other bins;
  • for every SpectralSet, diagnostic wavelength, and quantity of quantities, the optical depth of every bin and every size class in the layers (<q>3d_<λ>_<bin>, e.g. ext3d_550nm_Clay1) and of the column (<q>2d_<λ>_<bin>, e.g. ext2d_550nm_PM32Dust).

All variables are then processed like the variables of TimeSeriesMeSd (time series, multi-year means and standard deviations; gridded, zonal, and global means), with the settings of stats.

Required keyword arguments

  • stats::TimeSeriesConfig: runs, periods, calendar, seasons, time axis, vertical coordinate, optional pressure levels, threshold, products, and output settings (simpath, output_dir, output_name, global_attributes, …). Its groups and derived are not used and should be empty.
  • bins::Vector{DustBin}: size bins of the optical calculations, in the order of accumulation of the size classes.
  • input_dir::String: subdirectory of a run with the TimeSeriesMeSd output of the dust tracers (its output_dir, e.g. "Tracermass3d").
  • input_name::String: tag of these files (its output_name, e.g. "tracermass3d").

Optional keyword arguments

  • spectral::Vector{SpectralSet} = SpectralSet[]: spectral ranges, wavelengths, and lookup tables; empty: only effective radii.
  • fractions = nothing: fractions of the source tracer mass in the bins, one value per bin: a vector (all runs), a Dict from run identifier to vector, or a function run -> vector; nothing: all fractions 1. See bin_fractions. The fractions of the bins of one source should sum to 1.
  • input_root = nothing: directory with one subdirectory per run for the input; nothing: stats.simpath.
  • input_tag::String = "ijl": representation tag of the input files (gridded, model levels).
  • input_prefix = nothing: period prefix of the input file names (e.g. "JAN0001-DEC0020"); nothing: the single file matching *.<input_name><run>.<input_tag>.mts.nc.
  • airmass::String = "airmass": input variable with the air mass of the layers per unit area (kg m⁻²).
  • quantities::Vector{Symbol} = [:sct, :abs, :ext]: optical quantities, see OPTICAL_QUANTITIES.
  • layers::Bool = true: output the optical depths of the layers (3D).
  • columns::Bool = true: output the column optical depths (2D).
  • bin_variables::Bool = true: output the optical depths of the individual bins.
  • class_variables::Bool = true: output the optical depths of the size classes.
  • extrapolation::Symbol = :linear: extrapolation of the lookup tables, see interpolate_coefficients.
  • effective_radius_to_pressure::Bool = true: interpolate the effective radius of the layers to pressure levels when stats.pressure_levels is set. The optical depths of the layers are layer-integrated quantities and are never interpolated.

With stats.pressure_levels, the surface pressure (stats.pressure_levels. surface_pressure, e.g. prsurf) must be contained in the input files; it is read but not written.

Example

stats = TimeSeriesConfig(; simpath="/path/to/simulations", runs=["MarsDustRun1"],
    sim_period=(YearMonth(1, 1), YearMonth(20, 12)),
    stat_period=(YearMonth(3, 1), YearMonth(20, 12)), calendar=MARS_CALENDAR,
    seasons=MARS_SEASONS, output_dir="Traceroptics3d", output_name="traceroptics3d")
bins = [DustBin("Clay1"; source="Clay", radius=0.132e-6, density=2.5e3), …]
cfg = DustOpticsConfig(; stats=stats, bins=bins, spectral=[sw, lw],
    fractions=bin_fractions(bins, "Clay" => [0.009, 0.081, 0.234, 0.676]),
    input_dir="Tracermass3d", input_name="tracermass3d")

PlanetPostStats.DustOptics.column_mean_effective_radius — Method
column_mean_effective_radius(m::DustBinMasses, bins) -> Array{Float32,3}

Column average of the effective radius of the layers, weighted with the dust mass of the layers,

r̄_eff = Σ_l M_l r_eff,l / Σ_l M_l,     M_l = Σ_b m_b,l,

computed with PlanetTools.StatisticUtils.calc_weighted_mean over the vertical dimension: layers without dust or with missing values are ignored, columns without any valid layer are NaN32. Result (lon, lat, time).

In general this differs from the effective radius of the column dust load, effective_radius(column_masses(m), bins), which weights the layers with their particle cross sections instead of their masses.

PlanetPostStats.DustOptics.combine_bins — Method
combine_bins(m::DustBinMasses, coefficients) -> Array{Float32,4}

The linear combination Σ_b coefficients[b] · fractions[b] · masses[source of b] of the bin masses, computed per source tracer: the coefficients of all bins of one source are first summed, so every source array is read only once. Bins with a zero coefficient do not contribute, i.e. a missing value of their source does not propagate. Accumulation in Float32.

This one operation gives all fields of the optical post-processing:

  • optical depth of bin b or of a class of bins: coefficients k_b (mass extinction, scattering, or absorption coefficient, m² kg⁻¹) for the bins of the class, 0 otherwise;
  • total dust mass of the layer: coefficients 1;
  • numerator and denominator of the effective radius: 1/ρ_b and 1/(ρ_b r_b) (see effective_radius).

Arguments

  • m::DustBinMasses: the bin masses.
  • coefficients::AbstractVector{<:Real}: one coefficient per bin.

Example

m = DustBinMasses(["Clay", "Silt1"], [fill(1.0f0, 1, 1, 1, 1), fill(2.0f0, 1, 1, 1, 1)],
                  [1, 1, 2], [0.4, 0.6, 1.0])
combine_bins(m, [10.0, 20.0, 1.0])[1]      # 10 · 0.4 · 1 + 20 · 0.6 · 1 + 1 · 2 = 18
source
PlanetPostStats.DustOptics.effective_radius — Method
effective_radius(m::DustBinMasses, bins) -> Array{Float32,4}

Effective radius (m) of the size distribution given by the bin masses m of the size bins bins, in every grid cell and month,

r_eff = Σ_b V_b / Σ_b (V_b / r_b),     V_b = m_b / ρ_b,

the area-weighted mean radius of spherical particles [Hansen and Travis, 1974], with the effective radius r_b and the particle density ρ_b of every bin. This is the formula of PlanetTools.PlanetAerosolUtils.calc_size_integrated_effective_radius, evaluated for all grid cells at once with combine_bins. Cells without dust (zero total mass) and cells with a missing mass are NaN32.

Applied to column_masses(m), the result is the effective radius of the column dust load.

Arguments

  • m::DustBinMasses: bin masses (layer or column).
  • bins::AbstractVector{DustBin}: the bins, in the order of m.fractions.

References

  • Hansen, J. E., and L. D. Travis (1974), Light scattering in planetary atmospheres, Space Sci. Rev., 16(4), 527–610, doi:10.1007/BF00168069.

Example

bins = [DustBin("a"; radius=1e-7, density=2500), DustBin("b"; radius=1e-6, density=2500)]
m = DustBinMasses(["a", "b"], [ones(Float32, 1, 1, 1, 1), ones(Float32, 1, 1, 1, 1)],
                  [1, 2], [1.0, 1.0])
effective_radius(m, bins)[1]               # 2 / (1/1e-7 + 1/1e-6) ≈ 1.82e-7

PlanetPostStats.DustOptics.load_optics_run — Method
load_optics_run(cfg::DustOpticsConfig, coefficients, run) -> RunData

Reads the dust tracers and the air mass of run from its input_file and returns the RunData of the optical post-processing, whose variables are LazyVariables computed when they are written (see DustOpticsConfig).

Procedure

  1. Reads the months (year, month) of the file and keeps those within cfg.stats.sim_period, which must form a contiguous record.
  2. Reads the grid: lon, lat, the vertical coordinate of the tracers, the reference pressures plm and ple (if present), and the cell areas cfg.stats.area_variable.
  3. Reads the mass mixing ratios of the source tracers of cfg.bins (kg kg⁻¹) and the air mass cfg.airmass (kg m⁻²), and forms the dust mass per unit area of every source in every layer, q · m_air (DustBinMasses, with the fractions of run_fractions). Only these source masses are held in memory.
  4. With cfg.stats.pressure_levels, reads the surface pressure as auxiliary variable.

coefficients are the OpticalCoefficients of cfg.spectral (in order).

Example

coefs = [interpolate_coefficients(s, cfg.bins) for s in cfg.spectral]
rd = load_optics_run(cfg, coefs, "MarsDustRun1")
collect(keys(rd.variables))[1:3]

PlanetPostStats.DustOptics.optics_classes — Method
optics_classes(bins) -> Vector{Pair{String,Int}}

Cumulative size classes of the optical variables: pairs class => n, where the class class is the sum over the first n bins of bins.

The classes and their names are those of PlanetTools.DerivePhysicsVariables.accumulate_dust_optics_modele, for the bins Clay1, Clay2, Clay3, Clay4, Silt1, …, Silt5:

classsum of the bins
SumClay1DustClay1
SumClay2DustClay1 + Clay2
SumClay3DustClay1 + Clay2 + Clay3
PM2DustClay1 + … + Clay4
PM4Dust… + Silt1
PM8Dust… + Silt2
PM16Dust… + Silt3
PM32Dust… + Silt4
PM64Dust… + Silt5

As in PlanetTools, the accumulation stops at the first bin of bins that does not match this sequence, so that only complete classes are created; for bins with other names the result is empty.

Example

julia> names = ["Clay1", "Clay2", "Clay3", "Clay4", "Silt1"];

julia> bins = [DustBin(n; radius=1e-6, density=2500) for n in names];

julia> optics_classes(bins)
5-element Vector{Pair{String, Int64}}:
 "SumClay1Dust" => 1
 "SumClay2Dust" => 2
 "SumClay3Dust" => 3
      "PM2Dust" => 4
      "PM4Dust" => 5

PlanetPostStats.DustOptics.read_band_bounds — Method
read_band_bounds(path; header_row=3, band_column="Band") -> Vector{Tuple{Float64,Float64}}

Reads the wavelength bounds of the spectral bands from the CSV file path, with CSV.jl.

The column names are in line header_row of the file (the lines above are skipped, e.g. comments), followed by one line per band. Besides the column band_column with the band number, the file must have exactly two numeric columns, the lower and upper wavelength bound of the band (m); the order of the two does not matter. PlanetPostStats.DustOptics.run_dust_optics — Method
run_dust_optics(cfg::DustOpticsConfig; runs=cfg.stats.runs) -> Vector{RunResult}

Main entry point of the optical post-processing of dust. Validates cfg (validate_optics_config) and

  1. interpolates the optical properties of every spectral set to its diagnostic wavelengths and to the effective radii of the size bins (interpolate_coefficients) and writes them to the coefficients_file of the set, if one is given (write_coefficients);
  2. processes the runs one after the other with TimeSeriesMeSd.process_runs: reads the dust tracers and the air mass of the run (load_optics_run) and computes and writes every output variable, one at a time, with the time series and statistics of cfg.stats (output in output_directory(cfg.stats, run)).

Returns the RunResults of the runs (written files, summary tables of the global means of the 2D variables; see annual_summary, seasonal_summary).

Arguments

  • cfg::DustOpticsConfig: configuration.

Keyword arguments

  • runs = cfg.stats.runs: runs to process.

Example

results = run_dust_optics(cfg)
df = annual_summary(results)
filter(:Varname => ==("ext2d_550nm_PM32Dust"), df)
