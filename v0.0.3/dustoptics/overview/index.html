PlanetPostStats.DustOptics — Module
DustOptics

Submodule of PlanetPostStats for the optical post-processing of the dust aerosol of ModelE/ROCKE-3D simulations. Its input are the monthly time series of the dust tracer mass mixing ratios and of the air mass of the model layers written by TimeSeriesMeSd (e.g. *.ijl.mts.nc of the dust tracers Clay, Silt1, …). For every run and month it computes

  • the dust mass of every size bin of the radiative calculations in every layer, m_b = f_b q_s m_air, from the mass mixing ratio q_s of its source tracer, the fraction f_b of the tracer in the bin (e.g. the split of the clay tracer into four sub-bins), and the air mass m_air of the layer (DustBin, DustBinMasses);
  • the effective radius of the size distribution in the layers, of the column dust load, and its mass-weighted column average (effective_radius, column_mean_effective_radius);
  • the scattering, absorption, and extinction optical depths τ = k m_b of every bin and of the cumulative size classes (SumClay1Dust, …, PM2Dust, …, PM32Dust) in the layers and of the column, at selected diagnostic wavelengths, with the mass coefficients k interpolated from the optical lookup tables of the radiation code to the wavelengths and the effective radii of the bins (SpectralTable, SpectralSet, interpolate_coefficients);
  • the opacity profiles of every bin and size class in the layers, which, unlike the layer optical depth, can be interpolated to pressure levels: the opacity per kilometer β = τ ρ_air / m_air (km⁻¹, by default; opacity_per_km, with the air density air_density from the layer temperature and pressure), and, optionally, the density-scaled opacity τ / m_air (m² kg⁻¹; density_scaled_opacity).

All these variables are then written as time series, multi-year means, and standard deviations of gridded, zonal-mean, and global-mean fields with the pipeline of TimeSeriesMeSd (process_runs): one run at a time, and one variable at a time, since every variable is a LazyVariable computed from the bin masses only when it is written. The memory needed is that of the source tracer masses of one run plus a few fields.

All settings are collected in a DustOpticsConfig; the entry point is run_dust_optics.

Example

using PlanetPostStats.TimeSeriesMeSd
using PlanetPostStats.DustOptics

stats = TimeSeriesConfig(; simpath="/path/to/simulations", runs=["MarsDustRun1"],
    sim_period=(YearMonth(1, 1), YearMonth(20, 12)),
    stat_period=(YearMonth(3, 1), YearMonth(20, 12)), calendar=MARS_CALENDAR,
    seasons=MARS_SEASONS, vertical=VerticalCoordinate(31, 6.86, 0.2),
    output_dir="Traceroptics3d", output_name="traceroptics3d")
bins = [DustBin("Clay1"; source="Clay", radius=0.132e-6, density=2.5e3),
        DustBin("Clay2"; source="Clay", radius=0.23e-6, density=2.5e3),
        DustBin("Clay3"; source="Clay", radius=0.416e-6, density=2.5e3),
        DustBin("Clay4"; source="Clay", radius=0.766e-6, density=2.5e3),
        DustBin("Silt1"; radius=1.386e-6, density=2.65e3)]
sw = SpectralSet("sw", read_spectral_table("aer_sw_dust.nc", "sw_bands.csv"),
                 [0.55e-6, 0.89e-6])
cfg = DustOpticsConfig(; stats=stats, bins=bins, spectral=[sw],
    fractions=bin_fractions(bins, "Clay" => [0.009, 0.081, 0.234, 0.676]),
    input_dir="Tracermass3d", input_name="tracermass3d")
results = run_dust_optics(cfg)
annual_summary(results)
